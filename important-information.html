We don't collect or receive your data.

TabParcel copies, saves and opens the links you choose in your browser. It does not send your links to the developer or TabParcel servers.

Important information

What is restored

TabParcel saves and opens web addresses. It does not restore signed-in sessions, forms, cookies, individual tab history, groups, scroll positions or original windows. The TXT does not distinguish normal and incognito links; choose the destination when importing.

Review before opening

Links open only after you select them and request the action. Check domains in lists received from other people. HTTP and HTTPS are supported; this does not guarantee that a website is trustworthy. Internal pages and other protocols are excluded.

Limits and performance

Imports support up to 500 URLs and 1 MiB. More than 30 tabs require confirmation. Large imports use computer resources and can partially fail. Check the tabs already opened before retrying.

Keep your lists

Exported TXT files and the clipboard are controlled by you and the browser. Check the download before closing important pages. Use is voluntary and the extension is provided as is, without a guarantee of restoring content that is not part of a URL.
