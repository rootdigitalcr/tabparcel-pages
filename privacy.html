We don't collect or receive your data.

TabParcel copies, saves and opens the links you choose in your browser. It does not send your links to the developer or TabParcel servers.

Privacy

Data and storage

URLs, titles, pasted text and files are processed locally for your chosen actions. They remain in memory while the popup is in use. The opening process temporarily holds the requested URLs until it finishes. They are not sent to the developer or saved in a separate history.

Only language is retained

chrome.storage.local stores only the interface language, detected on first use or chosen by you later. It does not use sync storage. The detected language is saved on first use; your later choices are respected. Tabs, filters, selections and text are never saved there. Uninstalling the extension removes the preference.

Permissions

tabs reads titles and URLs when you open the popup, refresh, copy or download. downloads starts the local TXT download. clipboardWrite copies your selection; the clipboard is not read. storage retains only language. No website, history, cookie or page-content permissions are requested. Incognito requires manual authorization in Chrome.

Files, clipboard and opened pages

TXT files and copied links can contain private addresses or sensitive parameters. They remain under your control outside TabParcel; closing incognito or uninstalling does not delete them. Chrome maintains its normal download and browsing records. Opening imported links contacts the websites and uses the browser’s normal cookies and behavior.

Optional external links

There is no TabParcel telemetry, analytics, account or server. Ko-fi opens only when you choose Support on Ko-fi. The creator and More extensions links open root-cr.com and root-cr.com/extensions after a click, without a referrer. TabParcel attaches no links or preferences. Those websites have their own policies; Ko-fi handles payments and TabParcel does not receive payment results.

Limited Use

Information received from Chrome APIs is used in compliance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is used only to select, copy, export and import the requested links. It is not sold or used for advertising, profiling or human reading by the developer.

This site and public support

Documentation is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address under its own policy. This site adds no analytics, forms or cookies of its own. If you open a support request, GitHub processes what you choose to submit and the request is public. Do not attach private lists or tokens.
